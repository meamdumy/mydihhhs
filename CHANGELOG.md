# Changelog

## 1.1.0

- Bookmarks: hold a verse to bookmark it. Bookmarked verses are marked in the
  margin and listed under Bookmarks in the menu, where one can be opened or
  removed, with Undo.
- Theme: follow the device, or always light or always dark.
- Keep screen on: an option for reading without the display dimming.
- Dialogs and the Undo bar use the app's own colours throughout.

## 1.0.0

- Read the World English Bible offline: scroll through verses, swipe between
  chapters, and jump to any book and chapter from the side menu.
- Search for words or an exact phrase, or go straight to a reference such as
  John 3:16.
- Hold a verse to copy or share it.
- Adjustable text size, light and dark themes, and a text column that stays
  readable on tablets.
- Reopens where you stopped reading; Back returns to where you were after a
  jump.
