V2	-	Take words with you, and return to Yahweh.\nTell him, “Forgive all our sins,\nand accept that which is good:\nso we offer our lips like bulls.
V3	-	Assyria can’t save us.\nWe won’t ride on horses;\nneither will we say any more to the work of our hands, ‘Our gods!’\nfor in you the fatherless finds mercy.”
V4	-	“I will heal their waywardness.\nI will love them freely;\nfor my anger is turned away from him.
V5	-	I will be like the dew to Israel.\nHe will blossom like the lily,\nand send down his roots like Lebanon.
V6	-	His branches will spread,\nand his beauty will be like the olive tree,\nand his fragrance like Lebanon.
V7	-	Men will dwell in his shade.\nThey will revive like the grain,\nand blossom like the vine.\nTheir fragrance will be like the wine of Lebanon.
V8	-	Ephraim, what have I to do any more with idols?\nI answer, and will take care of him.\nI am like a green cypress tree;\nfrom me your fruit is found.”
V9	-	Who is wise, that he may understand these things?\nWho is prudent, that he may know them?\nFor the ways of Yahweh are right,\nand the righteous walk in them;\nBut the rebellious stumble in them.
